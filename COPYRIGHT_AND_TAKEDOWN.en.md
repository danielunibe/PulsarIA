# Copyright, Intellectual Property, and Takedown Requests

Version: 1.0 · September 12, 2026

Pulsaria respects copyright, trademarks, privacy, publicity, and other
applicable rights. User-imported content does not belong to Pulsaria and is
not published or redistributed from project servers.

## Takedown request

Send a request to the legal contact identified in the official version.
Include:

- your name and contact details;
- identification of the work or right allegedly affected;
- the URL or exact location of the Pulsaria-related material;
- an explanation of ownership or authorization;
- a good-faith statement;
- an accuracy statement;
- an electronic or physical signature where applicable.

Do not send personal data, cookies, credentials, full videos, or transcripts
to a public GitHub issue.

## Process

The rights holder will review the request, may ask for more information, may
limit distribution of an official artifact, or may remove material it
controls. A notice does not require Pulsaria to delete content outside its
control, such as an external URL or a file stored only on a user's device.

Abusive, incomplete, or misdirected requests may be rejected or referred to
the appropriate service.
