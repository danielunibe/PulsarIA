# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in the Pulsaria repository requires respectful, constructive,
and good-faith collaboration. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against,
impersonate, or expose private information about other people.

## Public project spaces

Keep issues, discussions, pull requests, and documentation focused on the
project. Do not publish credentials, cookies, private media, transcripts,
personal data, or content that you do not have permission to share.
